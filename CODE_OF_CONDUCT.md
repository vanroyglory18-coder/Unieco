# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We strive to make Unieco a welcoming, safe, and inclusive space for all students. We are committed to providing a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Offer help and constructive feedback.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Report any inappropriate behavior to moderators.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment and discrimination of any kind.
- Sharing private information without consent.
- Threats, bullying, or intimidation.
- Spam or malicious content.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the moderators or use the in-app reporting tools immediately.

Let's build a positive, supportive community together!
