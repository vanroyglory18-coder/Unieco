# Unieco

Unieco is a secure, friendly, location-based platform designed to connect tertiary students in hostels, campuses, and events. It enables students to interact, support each other’s needs, and engage in safe business—all within a stress-relieving, inclusive ecosystem.

## Vision
To create a digital community hub that empowers students to thrive academically, socially, and personally, reducing stress and fostering genuine connections.

## Core Features

- **Location-Based Groups:** Connect with students in your hostel, campus, or at events.
- **Needs & Offers Marketplace:** Request or offer help, sell or exchange goods/services securely.
- **Business Directory:** Discover and promote student-run businesses with trusted vendor badges.
- **Academic Support:** Join study groups, peer mentoring, and share resources.
- **Well-being & Events:** Access mental health support, find events, and join clubs for stress relief.
- **Privacy & Security:** Robust ID verification, encrypted messaging, and strong community moderation.
- **Rewards & Recognition:** Earn points and badges for community contributions.

## Roadmap

1. **MVP**
    - User authentication (with student verification)
    - Basic location-based chat/groups
    - Needs/offers posting & private messaging
    - Event listing and discovery
2. **Advanced Features**
    - Marketplace with secure payment
    - Trust badges & moderation tools
    - Academic resource sharing
    - Well-being and mental health integration
3. **Community & Growth**
    - Points/rewards system
    - Integration with campus facilities
    - Multi-language and accessibility support
